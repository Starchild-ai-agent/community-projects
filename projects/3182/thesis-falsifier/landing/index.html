Thesis Falsifier

Kill your bad investment ideas before they cost you money.

逆向证伪器 — a structured workflow that tries to destroy your thesis before you act on it.

See the workflow View source ↗
The problem

Most theses can't be wrong — so they're worthless.

"AI will drive growth" — can this ever be falsified? No. It's a narrative, not a thesis. It feels smart, it sounds specific, but there's no evidence that could prove it wrong. So it never gets tested. It just sits there, generating conviction without earning it.

"A thesis that survives an honest assassination attempt is worth acting on. One that doesn't survive was confirmation bias dressed up as analysis."

This skill's job: take your thesis, make it falsifiable, then try to kill it. If it lives — you act with real conviction. If it dies — you just saved yourself money.

Both outcomes win.

The workflow

Six steps from conviction to clarity.

1

证伪化改写 — Make it falsifiable

Break "buy X because Y" into three testable claims: Is Y actually true? Is Y already priced in? Will Y persist long enough to matter? Find the load-bearing claim — the one where if it's wrong, the whole thing collapses.

2

最弱环节 — Find the weak link

Of the three claims, which is most uncertain AND most checkable? That's the attack surface. Don't attack the strong claims — attack the weak one. A thesis is only as strong as its weakest load-bearing claim.

3

主动猎杀 — Active counter-evidence hunt

Run adversarial web searches: bear cases, short theses, "overvalued" takes, negative earnings surprises. Report what you find honestly. Finding nothing negative is itself a warning — either you didn't look hard enough, or the crowd hasn't noticed yet.

4

钢铁人对方 — Steel-man the bear

Write the strongest 3-sentence version of the opposite thesis. NOT a strawman — the version a smart bear would sign their name to. If it doesn't scare you a little, rebuild it.

5

代价量化 — Survival check

If the thesis is wrong: what's the max realistic downside? Can you survive being wrong at current position sizing? Define the invalidation signal upfront — what price or event proves the thesis wrong and forces exit?

6

证伪后信念 — Re-rate confidence

Re-rate your conviction 1–10 after all the above. The delta tells you what to do.

The verdict

Three outcomes. All of them useful.

↓ ≥3
Confidence dropped hard
Don't act. Confirmation bias.
↓ 1-2
Confidence dipped slightly
Proceed with eyes open.
→ or ↑
Confidence held or rose
Back it with real size.
Try it

One line to run it.

Install the skill, then tell your Starchild agent:

# Install npx skills add @3182/thesis-falsifier@1.0.0 # Then in any agent session: "I'm thinking of buying X because Y. Run the thesis falsifier on it."

The agent executes all six steps and returns a structured 证伪报告 — the claims, the counter-evidence, the steel-manned bear case, the survival math, and the re-rated confidence.

Works on any asset: stocks, crypto, macro bets, career decisions — anything with an "I should do X because Y" structure.
