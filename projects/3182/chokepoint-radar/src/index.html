Why these five? They are the points where geography forces all traffic through a single narrow corridor — so a localized event can reprice global freight, insurance and barrels in hours, not months.

03 — Context

How to read this

Flow numbers are order-of-magnitude estimates of daily oil transit, rounded and simplified for readability. They shift with seasons, OPEC policy and trade patterns — treat them as scale, not precision.

The disruption multiplier is a subjective rating of how badly a full closure would propagate: detour length, bypass capacity, and share of global flows.

The scenario slider is deliberately simple. Real price responses depend on spare capacity, inventories, and expectations — none of which a one-variable model can capture.
