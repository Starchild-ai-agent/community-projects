§01

The deal

Recruit a DEX onto Orderly. Orderly earns a taker fee from it. You take a cut of that fee, forever.

your payout = Orderly revenue (fees) × $ORDER staking tier

taker side only · no cap · no decay · no expiry

Orderly tier = the builder fee tier the same stake would earn on Orderly (Silver 100K, Gold 300K, Platinum 3M, Diamond 7M). Four of the six rungs sit exactly on Orderly's staking thresholds; the 1M rung is this program's own and has no Orderly counterpart.

Break-even = the taker volume that row has to produce before the extra 8% has paid back what the extra ORDER cost, with invitees on Public (3 bps). Cross it and the upgrade is free. The stake stays yours, subject to Orderly's 7-day unstaking period — not withdrawable on demand. If one invitee grows past $50M/mo its base fee falls to 2.75–1.00 bps, which pushes these break-even volumes higher.

Orderly's base taker fee is not one number — it falls with the invitee's own tier: 3.00 bps Public, 2.75 Silver (≥$50M/mo or 100K $ORDER), 2.50 Gold (≥$200M or 300K), 2.00 Platinum (≥$750M or 3M), 1.00 Diamond (≥$2B or 7M) — per Orderly trading fees. This calculator holds 3 bps flat, so at large single-invitee volumes it overstates revenue. Your percentage cut is unaffected.

§04

Rules

  • Entry is by application. BD approves on one test: can you actually deliver volume. Approved → 10%, live immediately, no stake required.
  • Perpetual, unconditional. Land one DEX and never work again — you keep that payout for the life of the DEX. Only fraud stops it.
  • Settlement is daily, at that day's rate, on the minimum stake held that day. Upgrades apply forward only.
  • One distributor per invitee, bound at or before graduation, immutable once set. No sub-distributors, no reciprocal pairs.
  • Sponsorship: you can put an invitee on cheaper pricing to win the deal. The discount comes out of your cut, so you decide whether it is worth it — no approvals.
§05

Cost

100 distributors is the success case, not the base case — $5B/mo of routed taker volume, a serious book for Orderly but a fraction of global perp flow. Everything scales linearly in volume: double the average book and every dollar figure doubles, while the 16% and the ORDER locked do not move.

Worst case is bounded at 50% by construction. The program is premised on paying only for flow a distributor originates; how much of that flow would have arrived anyway is an attribution question this model does not answer.

What this does not do

It is not a token sink. Even at 100 distributors the program locks 27M ORDER, about 6.6% of circulating — real, but not a supply story. Staking demand is bounded by distributor count, and a distributor who buys the top row once never needs to buy again no matter how large they grow. Judge this program on the revenue it originates, not on the ORDER it locks.
