§01

The deal

Recruit a DEX onto Orderly. Orderly earns a taker fee from it. You take a cut of that fee, forever.

your payout = share(ORDER staked) × Orderly's base-fee revenue

taker side only · no cap · no decay · no expiry
What v1 got wrong

v1 let you reach the top tier on invitee volume alone — the better you performed, the less reason you had to buy the token. v2 puts the rate on one dial and hands it to Orderly.

§04

Rules

  • Entry is by application. BD approves on one test: can you actually deliver volume. Approved → 10%, live immediately, no stake required.
  • Perpetual, unconditional. Land one DEX and never work again — you keep that payout for the life of the DEX. Only fraud stops it.
  • Settlement is daily, at that day's rate, on the minimum stake held that day. Upgrades apply forward only.
  • Staking above 7M buys nothing. 50% is the ceiling.
  • One distributor per invitee, bound at or before graduation, immutable once set. No sub-distributors, no reciprocal pairs.
  • Sponsorship: you can put an invitee on cheaper pricing to win the deal — the full discount comes out of your payout, floored at $0. Orderly is indifferent. Authority caps at Platinum.
  • Migration: internal BDs auto-enroll at 10%, bindings intact, paid max(v1, v2) for 90 days. Day 91 is v2 only.
Free consequence

With rate off volume, wash trading is strictly −EV. Faking volume makes the builder pay 3 bps and returns at most half of it. There is no tier left to farm — the whole abuse class from v1 disappears without a detection system.

The tradeoff this accepts

A flat ladder saturates. The whole thing tops out at $231,700, so a large distributor buys the top row once and never touches ORDER again. Locked supply grows by recruiting more distributors, not by any one of them scaling. That is the price of keeping volume off the rate.
